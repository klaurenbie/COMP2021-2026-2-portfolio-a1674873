FullTimeEmployee and Contractor can both be treated as Employee objects because they inherit from the Employee class.
Polymorphism allows the program to call the same methods, such as CalculatePay() and GenerateReport(), through an Employee reference. 
However, each subclass can provide its own implementation of these methods.
when the program calls CalculatePay(), it uses the version that belongs to the actual type of employee. 
For example, a full-time employee's pay is calculated from their salary, while a contractor's pay is calculated from their hours and hourly rate. 
This allows different types of employees to be handled consistently while still producing different results based on their specific implementations.
